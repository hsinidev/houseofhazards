### Game Details & Guide
Introducing House of Hazards



About the Game


The game House of Hazards is a browser-based puzzle game where you control a character navigating through a maze filled with obstacles and challenges.



How to Play


In this guide, we will cover how to play the game, including its controls and objective. To start, simply click on the "Play" button to begin your journey.



Controls


The controls are straightforward: use your mouse or keyboard to move the character left, right, up, or down within the maze boundaries.



To avoid obstacles, you will need to time your movements carefully. Obstacles will move towards you and can be avoided by pressing the "back" button in your browser.



Objective


The objective of the game is to reach the end of each level while avoiding obstacles and collecting power-ups.



Tips and Tricks for Success


To succeed, focus on navigating through the maze efficiently. Try to avoid obstacles as much as possible, especially at night when they are more aggressive.



Another crucial tip is to collect power-ups as soon as you can. These will give you temporary invincibility, speed boosts, or increased health.



Why You Should Play It


If you enjoy browser-based puzzle games with a unique twist, House of Hazards is the perfect game for you. The game's colorful graphics and catchy sound effects make it an enjoyable experience that will keep you coming back for more.



Additionally, the game offers a relaxing atmosphere, making it an excellent choice for those looking to unwind after a long day.



Additional Features


The game also features leaderboards, allowing you to compete with others to complete levels the fastest and with the most power-ups.
